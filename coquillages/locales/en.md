# Coquillages — EN

## Beneath the shell

Dive to the seabed and watch a shell grow.

## On the seabed.

This sea snail has a soft body. Its shell protects it.

## The snail gets bigger.

Its soft body grows. Its shell grows too, making room for the animal and keeping it protected.

## New shell is added at the edge.

The animal makes new material here. A tissue in its body does this. It is called the mantle.

The shell can also get thicker on the inside.

## Watch the shell grow.

Look at the opening: new shell is added here.

## What about a mussel?

Two shell halves protect its soft body. They grow at the edges too.

## A different shape. The same growth.

This shell gets bigger at the edge too. The earlier part stays in place.

## A shell grows with the animal.

The animal adds new material at the edge. The old part stays in place.

The animal makes its own shell. New shell is added at the edge.
